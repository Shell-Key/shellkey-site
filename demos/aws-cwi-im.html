AWS-CWI Workflow & Reference Library

Plain-language reference for welding inspection workflow, qualification traceability, examination, repairs, and reporting. The project-adopted construction code, specifications, approved procedures, and authorized engineering decisions remain controlling.

Important: AWS-CWI is an inspector credential and body-of-knowledge framework—not a universal construction acceptance code. This demo organizes records and workflow; it does not replace the project-adopted code, contract requirements, approved WPS/PQR/WPQ records, NDE interpretation, engineering evaluation, or inspector judgment.
